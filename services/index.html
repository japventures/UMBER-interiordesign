Services

Spaces designed around the people who use them. We connect concept, material choices and execution in a clear process, tailored to each project.

01

Interior design and remodeling

We develop a complete vision for new spaces and renovations: how rooms work, how they feel, and how materials and furnishings come together. We guide the design and coordinate its implementation with the specialists agreed for each project.

02

Commercial and hospitality design

We translate the identity of a restaurant, hotel or business into a spatial experience. We consider the customer journey, daily operations, lighting and material character to create spaces that work for both visitors and the team.

03

Bespoke furniture and styling

We give the interior its final character through custom pieces and a considered composition of furniture, textiles, lighting, art and objects. Each selection responds to the proportions of the space and the way it will be used.

Our process

A shared path with clear decisions at each step. The proposal sets the deliverables and timing for your project.

01

Discover and define

We begin with a conversation and a review of the space, your priorities and the way you live or work. Together we agree on the scope, investment framework and schedule before developing a design direction.

02

Design and refine

According to the agreed scope, we develop layouts and the look & feel for each space, 3D models, renders and virtual 3D tours. We select furniture, accessories and art, define materials and finishes, and design custom furniture. We review the look & feel with you before developing the 3D proposal, then record your feedback and approvals.

03

Plan and coordinate

With the design approved, we develop the agreed specifications and line-item budget. We coordinate suppliers, availability and delivery dates, keeping product selections, purchase arrangements and changes clearly documented.

04

Turnkey

For projects with turnkey service, we coordinate suppliers, installations, furniture deliveries and assembly. We handle site and quality issues with the responsible specialists and bring together furnishings, accessories and everyday essentials within the agreed scope, so you can arrive at a home that is ready to enjoy.
