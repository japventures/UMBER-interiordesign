SERVICES

Interior architecture

Scope and approach

We work on new-build interiors from the shell stage and on existing spaces to be remodeled. We develop the flooring and wall finishes, kitchens, bathrooms and custom cabinetry that define the interior.

The services, deliverables and stages are defined for each project. This establishes a clear basis for design decisions and coordination with the team.

Services

01

Interior architecture

Our core service. We develop residential and commercial interiors from the shell stage: interior layouts, flooring and wall finishes, kitchens, bathrooms and custom cabinetry, coordinated as one design proposal.

  • Interior layouts

    Organization of spaces, circulation and the relationship between rooms.

  • Flooring and wall finishes

    Selection of materials, finishes and their application throughout the interior.

  • Kitchen design

    Layouts, cabinetry, work surfaces and finishes developed together.

  • Bathroom design

    Selection and coordination of finishes, sanitary fixtures, faucets and accessories.

  • Custom cabinetry

    Design of closets, dressing rooms, built-in furniture and storage solutions.

  • Materials and interior details

    Coordination of finishes and the connections between the different elements of the space.

02

Interior design and furnishings

Selection of furniture, lighting, textiles, art and accessories, coordinated with the architecture and materials of each space.

03

Remodeling

Updating existing interiors through layout reviews, material selections and kitchen, bathroom and cabinetry design, according to the agreed scope.

04

Commercial and hospitality design

Interior architecture and design for restaurants and commercial spaces, considering their identity, circulation, operation and user experience.

The process

01

Project brief

We review the space, objectives and priorities to define the scope and direction of the project.

02

Interior architecture concept

We develop layouts and proposals for materials, kitchens, bathrooms and cabinetry.

03

Development and selections

We refine selections, dimensions and interior details with the client and project specialists. Drawings, specifications and other deliverables are defined in the proposal.

04

Coordination and installation

We coordinate the agreed scope with suppliers and specialists through installation and final review.
