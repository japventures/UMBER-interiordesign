Arethusa Residence

THE INTENTION

Arethusa connects the social and private areas of the home through symmetry and a considered material palette. The design treats each room as part of a wider composition, balancing continuity with the different rhythms of domestic life. The intention is a residential interior whose spaces relate clearly to one another without losing their individual character.
