Veneros Beach House

THE INTENTION

At Veneros Beach House in Punta Mita, the interior brings living, dining and kitchen spaces into a shared residential setting. Warm wood, woven surfaces and ceramic objects create depth against a restrained palette. The composition balances spaces for gathering with quieter places to pause, giving the home a relaxed character rooted in its coastal setting.
