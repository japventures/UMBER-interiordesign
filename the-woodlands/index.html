The Woodlands

THE INTENTION

This residence explores the balance between generous architectural proportions and the intimacy of everyday living. Custom furniture and a considered selection of art give each room its own focus while maintaining a coherent interior. The intention is to bring a human scale to the architecture through pieces, materials and relationships that feel composed rather than excessive.
