UMBER Interior Design

Interior architecture and design

Founded by Ana Paula de la Cerda and Melissa Arteaga, UMBER is an interior architecture and design studio based in The Woodlands, Texas, with residential and commercial projects in the United States and Mexico.

Our approach

Interior architecture is at the center of our work. From the shell stage, we develop kitchens, bathrooms, custom cabinetry, flooring and wall finishes. We coordinate these decisions with the furniture and the use of each space.

Our portfolio includes residences, apartments and hospitality spaces. Explore the projects to discover the materials and decisions behind each interior.
