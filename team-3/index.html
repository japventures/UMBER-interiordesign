UMBER INTERIOR DESIGN

The studio

A Legacy of Design and Vision.

UMBER Interior Design is a studio built on a foundation of professional excellence and decades of combined experience in the high-end residential and commercial industry. Founded by Ana Paula de la Cerda and Melissa Arteaga, our studio was born from a shared vision: to create spaces that transcend aesthetics and reflect the authentic essence of those who inhabit them.

Our Philosophy: Tailored for Authentic Living

We believe that great design is a balance of intention and intuition. Our approach is not guided by fleeting trends, but by harmony—meticulously balancing light, noble materials, and precise proportions. Our purpose is simple: to ensure your home or business evokes a sense of peace and a genuine connection from the moment you walk in.

Our Commitment: Excellence and Transparency

What defines UMBER is our disciplined and honest approach to project management. We understand that high-end design requires a seamless integration of creativity and logistical precision. We operate with total transparency, we provide real-time updates and clear communication, ensuring a trustworthy journey from the first sketch to the final reveal.

From our base in The Woodlands, Texas, and our extensive work across Mexico, we continue to transform visions into timeless environments, proving that true expertise is reflected in the lasting impact of the spaces we create.
