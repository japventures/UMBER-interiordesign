Frequently asked questions

Can you join a project at the shell stage?

Yes. We develop the interior architecture of new-build projects, including flooring and wall finishes, kitchen and bathroom design, custom cabinetry and interior selections. We also work on existing spaces to be remodeled.

What types of projects do you work on?

Our portfolio includes residences, apartments, restaurants and other hospitality spaces. The scope is defined for each project.

How is the project scope defined?

We review the location, type of space and client priorities. The proposal sets out the services, deliverables and stages to be included.

How are fees and purchasing terms established?

Fees, purchasing arrangements and any applicable supplier discounts are set out in the project proposal and agreement.

How is the schedule established?

The schedule depends on the agreed scope, design reviews, supplier lead times and site requirements. Dates are reviewed with the client during planning.

How will project information be shared?

The studio coordinates the review of proposals and deliverables with each client. The client portal is being prepared; access will be enabled once its configuration and tests are complete.

Where do you work?

The studio is based in The Woodlands, Texas, and its portfolio includes projects in the United States and Mexico. Contact us to review the location and scope of your project.
